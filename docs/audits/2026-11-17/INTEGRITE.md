## Confirmed missing primary fixture and malformed archive identities

The six deficient native editions were checked against all 596 returned native rows with two identified teams dated through 17 November. Forty-seven verification-source results fell on dates without native fixtures, without a matching score on an underfilled day or with fewer finished games. Of these, **36** have no identified native fixture on that date or either adjacent day. These signals overlap the six edition deltas and should not be summed with them. For a definitive example, the 2. Bundesliga opener was VfL Bochum 0–1 Hertha BSC on 7 August. The frozen Open Matchday file includes it, the competition owner's post-match report confirms it, and RDS has neither a nearby fixture nor a Bochum–Hertha pairing anywhere in the examined 2026/27 date range. The catalogue-present condition suppresses supplementary ingestion even in this case. First reconcile the native source or fill a genuinely absent canonical fixture using controlled fallback with explicit provenance. Scores and six aggregated source deficits do not automatically authorize creating player lines. [Native match witnesses](preuves/sources/upstream/native-result-witnesses.csv) · [Official league report](https://www.bundesliga.com/de/2bundesliga/news/vfl-bochum-1848-hertha-bsc-spieltag-1-spielbericht-highlights-38515) · [RDS six-edition query](preuves/db/verification_verified/17_native_result_shortfalls.json).

All twelve sampled archive cases with no recognized away team contain both actual sides and a score in the saved home name. In these cases a multi-line, tab-padded suffix such as “(Hinspiel 3:0)” defeats `dashboard/shared/archive.js:parseGoalMatch`. On failure `goalGroups` treats the complete source label as a team name; the archive writer can mint a club with that name. Calling the actual parser on the twelve production labels returns null for every case; collapsing whitespace in the same original string resolves both teams, score and suffix on all twelve. Reprocessing must preserve source labels, distinguish confirmed real teams from malformed synthetic identities, and verify that once-unmatched goal groups can now attach to existing canonical matches. Bounds for this evidence are 12 verified parser failures out of 1,783 affected historical records.
