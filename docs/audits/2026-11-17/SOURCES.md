## Historical validation and scoring implications

Across all 23 configured World Cups and all three EURO editions, independent retrieval of frozen upstream files found 1,222 expected fixture rows and 1,222 in RDS. The upstream scores establish 1,170 played fixtures against 505 marked finished in RDS. All 665 missing completed results are the alternate OpenFootball full-time array representation in 18 World Cups. In 1962 the entire 32-match competition appears unstarted. In 2022 England–USA's published 0–0 is genuinely known and must be retained. Sweden–Austria in 1938 was explicitly canceled, so a finished score is inapplicable. The remaining 51 future EURO 2028 fixtures have no published result. A digest of the unmodified provider response hides a parser-only backfill; normalization versions or a targeted re-run need to participate in the replay. Sample files contained scorer lists for 450 matches and no general lineup, substitutes, minutes or player-statistics data. Restoring the correct team results enables team algorithms, without implying eligibility of every player.

This validation covers the named OpenFootball editions only. Source counts use the immutable repository revisions and exact SHA-256 hashes listed in `upstream/reconciliation.csv`. RDS input is `../db/initial/editions.json`. Run `python3 upstream.py` from the evidence directory for a fresh-head comparison. Historical upstream data outside these editions and the full licensed PitchAPI catalogue remain independent completeness questions.

The open-matchday source manifest at `496e94ec4c976cbc244fc734c86e6772778a35f1` covers 75 folders. The European allowlist selects 65. Thirty supplied stored editions match RDS exactly, 4,705 unique fixtures including 2,601 completed results. The other 35 are covered by the explicit native mapping. All European source files downloaded and parsed, no duplicate result/fixture key appeared in this frozen snapshot and no provider folder reported errors. Per-file immutable URLs, checksums, distinct-key totals and differences are in `upstream/open-matchday-reconciliation.csv`, generated by `open-matchday-review.py`. The 35 mappings are an excellent opportunity to verify **actual** native coverage before dropping any future fallback. Presence of a native catalogue edition proves no more than the existence of the catalogue record.

`sources/identity.py:merge_teams` reassigns typed core team FKs, secondary keys and names, and keeps the losing row as a redirect. It does not rewrite generic `ops.provider_links.entity_id` or other non-core consumers in that pass. Preserve link history before retargeting any identity. `cups._team` and `open_matchday.save` give source-global name keys to teams whose providers do not have stable IDs. Identical clubs across countries, gender, age or historical successor must not be merged merely by a normalized name. Conversely, reversed team order with same score/date is a candidate not proof. Foreign key validity establishes referential integrity; it cannot establish real-world identity.

## Incremental ingestion, authority and fixes

1. Keep primary fixtures, supplemental results, official standings, enrichment, odds, sourced xG, and internal Vega player/team grades as separately owned facts. `core.player_match_stats.rating` is a provider statistic; Vega match grade is a separate output of the internal grade pipeline. A provider rating, a generated flag, a generic icon and a confirmed club badge must each retain honest provenance.
2. For Open Matchday, validate the manifest's per-folder expected counts and error flags, parse headers and accepted rows before changing an edition, and require consistent complete results before pruning. Explicit same-key finished results outrank scheduled observations. Legitimate fixture corrections/cancellations may remove superseded scheduled rows when the trusted complete snapshot supports the change. Preserve completed results and their stats across partial observations.
3. For cup feeds, reject empty snapshots that would wipe a previous edition and validate canceled/played states separately. Accept both published OpenFootball score encodings and preserve explicit cancellation. Make changes in parsing reliably replay a stored edition whose underlying payload remains identical. The published edition digest currently covers name and matches only.
4. Scope changes in existing active Linear areas under VI-21 (identity/ingestion/Elo), VI-19 (cup prediction), VI-22 (club interface), VI-23 and VI-26 (calculations and freshness). `power.elo_matches` skips all 35 COVERED folders unconditionally, while the importer checks whether the specific season actually has a native edition. Audit a newly added fallback season end to end before accepting this mismatch.
5. Separate integrity evidence from coverage counts. `ops.source_runs` stores latest attempt/success and per-scope row/unmatched counters. It is not an immutable history of individual upstream requests. Retrying and hydration produce legitimate repeated job targets; repeated request evidence needs URL/family/page, job and time to establish avoidable calls.

The complete configured source/scope intervals and finite mappings are in `configured-scopes.csv` and `precedence-mappings.csv`. `sql/01…15` contains the read-only queries. Snapshot coordinates, query hashes and timestamps are in the separate `../db/*/receipt.json` files.
